Recurring issue clusters

  1. Splash / first-screen hang after update App stalls on the opening screen after installing or updating.
  2. Android Developer Mode / device allow-list Some phones are blocked when Developer Options are enabled or the device is not on the approved list.
  3. OTP / login / password reset codes never arrive SMS or email verification codes do not show up, blocking sign-in or password recovery.
  4. Forced nearest store, cart wipe, false out-of-stock App switches to a nearer store without clear consent, empties the cart, or marks items unavailable incorrectly.
  5. Checkout stuck after pay / card never saves Payment appears to process but the order stays on “processing,” or saved cards fail to stick.

Search also often misses products unless you type the exact brand name — a separate usability complaint seen across reviews.

What you should do

If you are a customer

  • 1
    Splash screen hangs after an update

    Try uninstalling and reinstalling the app. If it still hangs, contact us through in-app chat with your phone model and app version.

  • 2
    Blocked because of Developer Options

    This is a known limitation on some Android devices. We are working on a fix. Turning off Developer Options may let you proceed in the meantime.

  • 3
    OTP or password-reset code never arrives

    Check your email as well as SMS, wait a few minutes, and avoid requesting multiple codes in a row. If nothing arrives, use in-app chat so we can verify your account.

  • 4
    Checkout shows “processing” after you paid

    Do not pay again. Note the time and amount, then contact in-app chat with your order details so we can confirm payment status.

  • 5
    Store changed and your cart was emptied

    Tell us through in-app chat which store you wanted and what items were in your cart. We will help you rebuild the order.

For staff: Keep prioritising fixes for the five clusters above. Do not post public store replies until responses are confirmed and approved.
